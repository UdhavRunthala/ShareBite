## Summary
Expose proof-backed impact and recognition alongside searchable community profiles. Pages can now consume database-derived metrics, rankings, certificates and demand requests.

## Changes
- Donor/NGO leaderboards with India-calendar month and all-time filters.
- Impact series and explicitly estimated food-weight/CO2 metrics.
- Public certificate verification, private profile/document management and NGO directory.
- Demand requests and scoped reporting of listings, users and messages.

## How to test
- Compare leaderboard meals against completed-order proof totals.
- Fetch a certificate by ID and confirm no private user fields appear.
- Submit profile documents, demand requests and reports with the relevant role.

## Screenshots / notes
